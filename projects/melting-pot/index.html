Melting Pot

How might we help people explore unfamiliar cultures through the food they already love?

Overview

Melting Pot is a mobile experience built around cultural food discovery. Working in a small team, I led interface design and prototyping — translating early research into a warm, approachable product that lowers the barrier to trying new cuisines and learning the stories behind them.

Role

UI/UX Design, Prototyping

Timeline

Team project · 6 weeks

Tools
FigmaFigJamUser Interviews

Understanding the problem

We started with interviews and affinity mapping to understand how people decide what to eat. A recurring theme emerged: curiosity about other cultures was often blocked by uncertainty — not knowing what a dish was, how to order it, or where to start. We reframed the challenge around building confidence, not just listing restaurants.

Designing the experience

The final flow pairs a browsable discovery feed with short cultural context for each dish, so trying something new feels guided rather than intimidating. A consistent card system, warm accent colors, and clear hierarchy keep the interface friendly and easy to scan on the go.
