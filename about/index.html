Hello!

My name is Eunice Kim (Yoo-niss), but my friends call me Euni. I am an Interactive Design student at James Madison University.

Growing up, I would often sketch or create pieces ranging from crocheting to patchwork on shirts while spending my time scrolling through Pinterest for inspiration. Naturally, this has led me to follow a path that combines both design and technology.

My goal is to gain experience, improve my UI/UX design skills, and encounter different perspectives and situations in a professional setting. I aspire to connect my work with others and find my own style that stands out.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me eating out, skiing, playing the guitar, and making vlogs with my friends!
